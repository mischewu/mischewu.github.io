Quantitative sociologist and family demographer studying how family and institutional contexts combine and develop over time to produce inequalities in educational and socio-demographic trajectories. I study education both as a predictor of inequalities in the early family life course and as an outcome of childhood circumstances, and I work on adapting longitudinal, person-centred methods to population-scale register data.

PhD candidate at Erasmus University Rotterdam (2023–27) and LIVES, the Swiss Centre of Expertise in Life Course Research (2023–26). I am supervised by [Tom Emery](https://tomemery.eu) and [Alzbeta Bartova](https://www.universiteitleiden.nl/en/staffmembers/alzbeta-bartova), and part of the team working on the [ERC Childcare Strategies](https://www.eur.nl/en/news/erc-starting-grant-tom-emery-research-childcare-and-inequality) project.

I completed the [MPPSC at the University of Tübingen](https://uni-tuebingen.de/fakultaeten/wirtschafts-und-sozialwissenschaftliche-fakultaet/faecher/fachbereich-sozialwissenschaften/politikwissenschaft/studium/studium/master/master-of-public-policy-and-social-change-ma/) in 2023, and am on leave from the [MA Philosophy & Economics at the University of Vienna](https://ssc-phil.univie.ac.at/studien/master-philosophy-and-economics/).
